## How It Works

### **Phase 1: Your Brand Setup** (5 minutes)
- Answer a smart quiz about your podcast (name, listener count, sports focus, brand values)
- System captures your 7-layer brand culture (using Brainwash OS framework)
- Auto-generates campaign contexts so all outreach feels authentically *you*

### **Phase 2: Intelligent Discovery** (Runs daily, hands-off)
- Automated web scraping finds relevant contacts across all 4 campaigns
- Phone/email verification ensures contact accuracy
- Relevance scoring surfaces the best prospects first
- Results sync to your dashboard and integrations (Slack, Sheets, etc.)

### **Phase 3: Adaptive Script Generation** (Runs with discovery)
- AI generates 3–5 outreach script variants per campaign
- Scripts incorporate your brand voice and values
- After first 30 attempts, scripts auto-adapt based on what converts
- You get weekly insights: "This angle converts 3x better than that one"

### **Phase 4: Campaign Execution** (Fully automated)
- Batch outreach runs daily (20 contacts per campaign per day, configurable)
- Outcomes logged: booked, declined, no answer, follow-up needed
- Database tracks everything for transparency and learning
- Zero intervention needed

### **Phase 5: Smart Monitoring & Integrations** (Your dashboard)
- Real-time campaign status (prospects contacted, conversion rate, pipeline)
- Weekly digest emails/Slack messages (no daily noise, one summary)
- GitHub-based approval workflow for recommended integrations
- Manual override buttons anytime you want to jump in
